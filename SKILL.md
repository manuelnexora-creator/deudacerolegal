Key things to remember for Deuda Cero Legal:
- Spanish (Spain) copy always — use **tú**, never *usted*.
- Empathetic authority tone: the audience is financially stressed. Gravitas over hype. No emojis, no exclamation stacks.
- Palette: Oxford Blue `#1B263B` primary · Gold `#E0B458` accent · Coral `#F28482` CTA only · Emerald `#1E6B47` from logo (success states). White and deep navy `#0B1622` surfaces only — no midtone gray backgrounds.
- Montserrat 700–900 for display, Inter for body.
- Lucide icons, stroke 1.75. Never fill.
- Single CTA per screen. Coral only for CTAs — nothing else.
- Mobile-first; most traffic is from ads on phones.
- Always use the `¿…?` inverted question mark — it's a signature.
